The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB) conducts the **Informatics Assistant (Suchna Sahayak - IA)** typing test.

This exam is highly technical, and candidates must clear the computer-based typing speed test in both Hindi and English. The test is qualifying in nature, meaning your scores will not be added to the final merit list, but passing both sections is mandatory to secure the post. You can **[practice with Rajasthan IA mock exams](https://typingnexus.in)** for free.

Here is a comprehensive breakdown of the speed thresholds, keyboard layouts, backspace rules, and evaluation criteria for the Rajasthan Informatics Assistant typing test.

---

## 1. Speed & Duration Requirements

Candidates must qualify in both typing sessions:

*   **English Typing Speed**: Minimum **20 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Hindi Typing Speed**: Minimum **20 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Test Duration**: **15 minutes** for each language.
*   **Time Gap**: A break of **10 to 15 minutes** is typically provided between the Hindi and English typing sessions.
*   **Target Words**: You must type a minimum of **300 correct words** in each 15-minute session to qualify.

---

## 2. Keyboard Layout and Font Choices

The exam environment uses Unicode fonts for Hindi, offering multiple layout options:

*   **Hindi Font**: **Unicode Mangal Font** is mandatory.
*   **Hindi Keyboard Layout**: Candidates are generally given a choice between:
    1.  **Remington Gail Keyboard Layout** (recommended for those who have typewriter or Krutidev background).
    2.  **Inscript Keyboard Layout** (standard government layout).
*   **English Layout**: Standard QWERTY layout.

---

## 3. Backspace and Navigation Rules

Unlike the strict RSMSSB LDC exam, the Informatics Assistant typing rules are slightly more lenient:

*   **Backspace is Allowed**: The backspace and delete keys are fully functional during both sessions. You are permitted to correct errors.
*   **Arrow Keys**: Left/Right and Up/Down arrow keys are enabled to help navigate the typed text.
*   **Standard Interface**: The source text is displayed on screen, and candidates type in a text box directly below the passage.

> [!TIP]
   > With a 15-minute duration, the test evaluates stamina. Do not rush and exhaust your fingers in the first 5 minutes. Maintain a steady, comfortable rhythm since the target speed (20 WPM) is relatively moderate.

---

## 4. Evaluation and Speed Calculation

RSMSSB calculates speed using the net correct words typed over the 15-minute duration.

**Net Speed Formula:**
```text
Net Speed (WPM) = (Total Words Typed - Incorrect Words) / 15 Minutes
```

To pass, your calculated **Net Speed** must be at least 20 WPM in both languages. Excessive uncorrected errors will lower your net speed and can lead to disqualification.

---

## 5. Tips to Clear the Rajasthan IA Typing Test

1.  **Build Stamina for 15 Minutes**: Typing continuously for 15 minutes can lead to finger fatigue. Practice full 15-minute timed sessions at home to build finger endurance.
2.  **Focus on Accuracy**: The speed requirement is 20 WPM, which is very achievable. Do not compromise accuracy for excessive speed; keep errors to a minimum to ensure your net speed stays above 20 WPM.
3.  **Use Desktop Keyboards**: Ensure you practice using a standard membrane desktop keyboard. Soft laptop keyboards do not replicate the deep key travel of exam center keyboards.
4. Also check the **[RSMSSB LDC typing test rules](/blog/rssb-ldc-typing-test-rules)** for the larger clerk-level exam in Rajasthan which uses a strict word-locking backspace rule.
