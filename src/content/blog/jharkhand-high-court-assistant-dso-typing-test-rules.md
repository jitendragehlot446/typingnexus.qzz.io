The **High Court of Jharkhand (Ranchi)** conducts recruitment for **Assistant / Clerk, Deputy Section Officer (DSO), and English Typist / Deposition Typist** posts.

Candidates who clear the preliminary written examination must appear for the mandatory **Computer Skill Test / Typing Test (20 Marks) and Computer Proficiency Test (CPT)**.

In this guide, we break down the exact speed requirements, evaluation criteria, and practical formatting tasks for Jharkhand High Court exams.

## 1. Speed & Keystroke Calculation

* In a 10-minute English test at 40 WPM, candidates must type at least **400 words (2000 Key Depressions)**.
* Passages are typically legal orders, civil writs, or criminal appeal summaries.
* **Typing Marks Weightage**: In Jharkhand High Court, typing marks are NOT just qualifying; they are **added directly to your Mains written score** to determine the final selection rank!

---

## 2. Computer Proficiency Test (CPT) Syllabus

Along with the typing speed test, candidates must complete a practical CPT module:

1. **MS Word**: Paragraph alignment, table creation, line spacing (1.5 lines), bold/italic/underline formatting, and page numbering.
2. **MS Excel**: Basic formulas (`SUM`, `AVERAGE`, `IF`, `COUNT`), border creation, and cell alignment.
3. **Internet & Emailing**: Composing emails, attaching files, and searching court orders online.
