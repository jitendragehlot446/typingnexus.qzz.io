The **High Court of Gujarat (Ahmedabad)** conducts recruitment for **Computer Operator (IT Cell), English Stenographer Grade-II/III, and Assistant / Clerk** posts.

Candidates must clear a rigorous **Computer Typing Speed Test and Practical Computer Proficiency Test (CPT)** to qualify for the appointment list.

In this guide, we explain the speed standards, Gujarati and English font requirements, evaluation scheme, and preparation tips for Gujarat High Court.

## 1. Speed Calculation & CPT Evaluation

* For Computer Operator: Candidates are given an on-screen technical/legal passage of **400 words in English (10 minutes)**.
* For Gujarati Typist / Assistant: Passages are provided in Gujarati Unicode fonts (*Shruti / Gujarati Indic Input 3*).
* **Practical CPT Modules**: Formatting legal writs in MS Word, formula computations in MS Excel, and file management in Windows.
