The Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) conducts a mandatory **Typing Skill Test** for the recruitment of Junior Secretariat Assistants (JSA), formerly known as Lower Division Clerks (LDC).

The typing test is qualifying in nature but essential to clear to secure your final job offer. Below is a detailed breakdown of the speed thresholds, keyboard layouts, backspace rules, and preparation strategies.

---

## 1. Speed and Duration Requirements

*   **English Typing Speed**: Minimum **35 WPM** (Words Per Minute).
*   **Hindi Typing Speed**: Minimum **30 WPM** (Words Per Minute).
*   **Duration**: **10 minutes**.
*   **Keystroke Calculation**: Speeds correspond to approximately **10,500 key depressions per hour (KDPH)** for English and **9,000 KDPH** for Hindi (based on an average of 5 key depressions per word).

---

## 2. Keyboard Layout and Font Specifications

KVS exams typically use legacy font structures for Hindi typing tests:

*   **English**: Standard QWERTY layout.
*   **Hindi Font**: **Kruti Dev 010** is the standard font.
*   **Hindi Layout**: Standard **Remington Keyboard Layout**.
*   *Note:* Ensure you configure your Hindi settings to Kruti Dev (or DevLys 010) during your preparation, as typing on Unicode Mangal fonts behaves differently.

---

## 3. Backspace and System Navigation

*   **Backspace is Allowed**: You **are permitted** to use the backspace and delete keys to correct mistakes during the test.
*   **Passage Interface**: The passage is displayed in one box on screen, and you must type the text in the provided input field below it.
*   **Warm-Up Demo**: A short **2-minute trial/mock test** is usually provided before the actual test starts to allow candidates to check the keyboard keys. If any keys are sticky or malfunctioning, request the invigilator for a hardware change during this trial period.

---

## 4. Evaluation and Calculation Formula

The test is qualifying, meaning you must achieve the minimum speed to pass, but your score is not added to the final merit list:

*   **Net WPM Calculation**:
    
    ```text
    Net Speed = [Total Keystrokes / 5 - Mistakes] / 10 Minutes
    ```
    
*   **Qualifying Threshold**: Minimum net speed of 35 WPM (English) or 30 WPM (Hindi).

---

## 5. Tips to Pass the KVS JSA Typing Test

1.  **Use the Trial Test Wisely**: Press every key on your keyboard during the 2-minute mock run to ensure spacebar, backspace, and letter keys function smoothly.
2.  **Aim for High Accuracy**: Net speed is calculated by deducting mistakes. Practice maintaining an accuracy rate of **95%+** to ensure your final net speed does not drop below the qualifying mark.
3.  **Practice on Physical Keyboards**: Do not practice only on soft laptop keys. Practice on standard membrane desktop keyboards, as these are used in KVS centers. You can practice in real exam mode on the **[Typing Nexus Test Simulator](https://typingnexus.in)**.
