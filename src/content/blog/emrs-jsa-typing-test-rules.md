The **Emrs Jsa** typing test is a strict qualifying stage for candidates aiming to secure a clerical post. This article breaks down the exact Words Per Minute (WPM), backspace rules, and layout options you need to know.

---

## 1. Typing Speed and Duration Rules

According to the official notification, candidates must achieve a minimum English typing speed of **30 WPM** and a Hindi typing speed of **25 WPM**. The test is typically conducted for exactly **10 mins** on a computer.

---

## 2. Typing Interface and Layouts

The exam interface provides a standard QWERTY layout for English. For Hindi, the officially mandated font is **Mangal Unicode**. Ensure you practice on the Remington Gail layout as it is the most commonly provided option.

---

## 3. Rules on Accuracy and Mistakes

The marking scheme heavily penalizes errors. While backspace is enabled, relying on it too much is dangerous. Any misspelled word or extra space counts as an error, so strive for 95%+ accuracy.
