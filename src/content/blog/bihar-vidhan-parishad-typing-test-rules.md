The **Bihar Vidhan Parishad** recruits candidates for Lower Division Clerk (LDC) positions. The typing skill test is a mandatory qualifying round.

Here are the official typing test rules for the Bihar Vidhan Parishad LDC post.

---

## 1. Speed Requirements

*   **English Speed Requirement**: **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Hindi Speed Requirement**: **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Duration**: Typically **10 Minutes**.

---

## 2. Keyboard and Layout Options

*   **English**: Standard QWERTY layout.
*   **Hindi**: **Mangal Font** with the **Remington Gail** keyboard layout.

---

## 3. Backspace and Correction Rules

*   **Backspace Allowed**: You are allowed to use the backspace key to correct errors. 
*   **Error Limit**: Maintaining high accuracy (above 95%) is crucial. Errors will be deducted from your gross speed to calculate your final Net WPM.
