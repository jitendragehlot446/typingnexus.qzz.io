The **Bihar Vidhan Sabha** conducts recruitment for various clerical posts, including LDC and Data Entry Operators. The typing test is strictly qualifying.

---

## 1. Speed and Duration Rules

*   **English Typing**: Minimum speed of **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Hindi Typing**: Minimum speed of **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Test Duration**: Exactly **10 minutes**.

---

## 2. Font and Keyboard Layouts

*   **English**: Standard QWERTY keyboard.
*   **Hindi**: Hindi typists must type using the **Unicode Mangal Font**.
*   **Hindi Layout**: Candidates use the **Remington Gail** keyboard layout.

---

## 3. Test Interface and Backspace

*   **Backspace**: Backspace is **permitted** to correct errors.
*   **Errors**: Missing words, spelling mistakes, and punctuation errors carry severe penalties. Focus on 100% accuracy.
