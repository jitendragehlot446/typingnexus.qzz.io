The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSMSSB) conducts a highly challenging **LDC Typing Test** for the recruitment of Lower Division Clerks and Junior Assistants.

This test is merit-based, meaning the marks you score in typing are added to your overall written score. It is also famous for its strict **word-locking backspace rule** and specific legacy font requirements.

---

## 1. Speed and Marks Distribution

The exam has separate sessions for Hindi and English. Each session consists of a speed test and an efficiency test:

*   **English Speed Test**: **35 WPM** target (10 minutes, 25 Marks).
*   **Hindi Speed Test**: **30 WPM** target (10 minutes, 25 Marks).
*   **Efficiency Test**: Tests your MS Office skills (10 minutes, 25 Marks for each language).
*   **Total Typing Phase Marks**: **100 Marks** (added to final merit!).

---

## 2. The Strict "Word-Locking" Backspace Rule

Unlike central government tests, RSMSSB implements a strict editing limit:

*   **Word-Locked Backspace**: You can use backspace to edit and correct characters in the **current word** you are typing.
*   **No Edits After Space**: The moment you press the `Spacebar` or `Enter` key, the word is locked. You cannot go back to correct a locked word.
*   **Arrow keys and mouse click** are completely disabled in the typing area.

---

## 3. Keyboard Layout and Fonts

*   **Hindi Font**: **DevLys 010** is the legacy font used.
*   **Hindi Keyboard Layout**: **Remington Gail** layout. Unicode (Mangal/Inscript) is not used.
*   **English Font**: Calibri or Courier New on standard QWERTY layout.
*   **Shortcut Restrictions**: Alt key combinations and Ctrl-shortcuts are disabled.

---

## 4. Tips for RSMSSB LDC Typing

1.  **Stop Backspacing Out of Habit**: Because you can't correct a word once you press space, focus on typing the word correctly on the first attempt.
2.  **Practice on DevLys 010**: Practicing on Mangal or Unicode fonts will not help. Ensure you practice typing on the legacy **DevLys 010** font.
3.  **Simulate Word Locking**: You can **[practice with word-locking rules turned on](https://typingnexus.in)** for free.
4.  **Efficiency Test Prep**: Do not ignore the efficiency test. Learn basic MS Word and MS Excel formatting, margins, tables, and borders, as they carry a massive 50 marks.
