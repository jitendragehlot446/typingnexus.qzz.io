The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) conducts the **CEPTAM (Centre for Personnel Talent Management)** exam for Admin & Allied cadre posts (such as Admin Assistant, Store Assistant).

The selection process includes a mandatory computer-based **Typing Test** for clerical positions. The test is qualifying in nature, meaning your typing score won't be added to the final merit list, but passing it is mandatory to secure your selection. You can **[practice with DRDO CEPTAM mock exams](https://typingnexus.in)** for free.

Below is a detailed breakdown of the DRDO CEPTAM typing speed requirements, keyboard layouts, backspace rules, and evaluation criteria.

---

## 1. Speed & Duration Requirements

Candidates can choose to take the test in English or Hindi:

*   **English Typing Speed**: Minimum **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Hindi Typing Speed**: Minimum **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Test Duration**: **10 minutes**.
*   **Target Keystrokes**: To qualify, you must achieve approximately **10,500 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH)** in English or **9,000 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH)** in Hindi.
*   **Word Calculation**: In official speed calculations, 1 word is defined as **5 key depressions** (including spaces and punctuation).

---

## 2. Keyboard Layout and Font Rules

The exam environment provides standard configurations for both languages:

*   **English Layout**: Standard QWERTY keyboard layout.
*   **Hindi Font**: **Unicode Mangal Font** is mandatory.
*   **Hindi Keyboard Layout**: Candidates are generally given a choice between:
    1.  **Inscript Keyboard Layout** (standard government layout).
    2.  **Remington Gail Keyboard Layout** (compatible with typewriter layouts).

---

## 3. Backspace and Navigation Rules

*   **Backspace is Allowed**: You can use the backspace key and delete keys to edit and correct typos during the 10-minute test.
*   **Arrow Keys**: Left and right arrow keys are enabled to help navigate the typed text.
*   **Split-Screen Interface**: The source passage is displayed in the upper box on the screen, and you must type the text in the text input box in the lower half.

> [!WARNING]
   > While backspace is permitted, using it too frequently will slow down your speed. Focus on typing accurately on your first attempt. Every correction costs valuable seconds.

---

## 4. Evaluation and Net Speed Calculation

DRDO CEPTAM evaluates candidates based on **Net Speed (WPM)**, which deducts penalties for uncorrected errors from the gross speed.

**Net Speed Formula:**
```text
Net Speed (WPM) = [(Total Keystrokes Typed - Errors Penalty) / 5] / 10 Minutes
```

To qualify, your **Net Speed** must be at least 35 WPM in English or 30 WPM in Hindi. If you type fast but commit too many uncorrected errors, your net speed may fall below the threshold, leading to disqualification.

---

## 5. Tips to Pass the DRDO CEPTAM Typing Test

1.  **Aim for a Safety Margin**: Aim for a speed of **40+ WPM in English** or **35+ WPM in Hindi** during your practice sessions at home. This will provide a buffer against exam-day nerves and unfamiliar keyboards.
2.  **Practice on Membrane Keyboards**: Exam centers use standard membrane desktop keyboards with deep key travel. Avoid practicing exclusively on soft laptop keyboards.
3.  **Focus on Accuracy First**: Try to keep your accuracy above 95%. This minimizes the need to use the Backspace key, saving time and keeping your typing rhythm steady. You can **[practice with timed exam-like paragraphs](https://typingnexus.in)** to monitor your progress.
