## 1. Speed & Keystroke Calculation

* The examination is conducted on computer terminals.
* In a 10-minute test at 40 WPM, you must accurately type a minimum of **400 standard words (2000 Key Depressions)**.
* Passages are typically drafted from actual court judgments, legal petitions, statutory notifications, and administrative circulars.

---

## 2. Strict 3% Mistake Permissible Limit

Delhi District Courts enforce one of the tightest accuracy standards in India:

* For a 400-word passage, a 3% error limit allows a maximum of **12 mistake points**.
* If a candidate achieves 45 WPM but makes 15 mistakes (3.75% error), they are **DISQUALIFIED**.
* **Full Mistakes**: Omitted words, added words, or substitution of legal terminology.
* **Half Mistakes**: Spelling errors, incorrect capitalization of proper nouns (e.g. *Hon'ble Court, Petitioner, Respondent*), and missing commas/colons.

---

## 3. Pro Tips to Clear Delhi District Courts JJA Typing Test

1. **Practice Legal & Formal Passages**: Practice paragraphs containing legal terms such as *Jurisdiction, Affidavit, Appellant, Interlocutory, Quash, and Supreme Court*.
2. **Master Capitalization and Punctuation**: Court judgments have strict semicolon, hyphen, and quotation mark rules.
3. **Aim for 45+ WPM at Home**: Because 40 WPM is the bare minimum pass mark, maintaining a 48 WPM cruising speed at home ensures comfortable qualification under exam hall pressure.
