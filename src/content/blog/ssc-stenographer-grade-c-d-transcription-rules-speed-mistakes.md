## 2. Full Mistakes vs Half Mistakes Evaluation Rules

SSC evaluates computer transcription with precise mistake point penalties:

### A. Full Mistakes (1.0 Error Point Deducted):
1. **Omission**: Leaving out any word or figure dictated in the audio.
2. **Substitution**: Replacing a dictated word with a different word (e.g. typing *"nation"* instead of *"country"*).
3. **Addition**: Typing extra words not present in the original dictation.

### B. Half Mistakes (0.5 Error Point Deducted):
1. **Spelling Errors**: Typing wrong spelling or transposing letters.
2. **Capitalization**: Using lowercase for proper nouns or uppercase in mid-sentence.
3. **Punctuation & Paragraphs**: Missing full stop (`.`) or failing to insert mandatory paragraph breaks.
4. **Singular/Plural Confusion**: Typing *"student"* instead of *"students"*.

> **Total Mistake Calculation**:  
> `Total Errors = Full Mistakes + (Half Mistakes × 0.5)`  
> `Error Percentage (%) = (Total Errors / Total Dictated Words) × 100`

## 3. Pro Tips to Clear SSC Stenographer Skill Test

1. **Maintain 35+ WPM Hindi / 40+ WPM English Typing Speed**: If your computer typing speed is slow, you will run out of transcription time even if your shorthand notebook is flawless.
2. **Read Back Your Shorthand Daily**: Practice reading your handwritten outlines aloud without hesitation.
3. **Proofread in the Last 5 Minutes**: Always leave 5 minutes to verify spelling, numbers, and capitalization against your original shorthand notes.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: What is the dictation speed for SSC Stenographer Grade C and Grade D?**  
**Answer**: Grade C requires **100 WPM** (1,000 words in 10 minutes) and Grade D requires **80 WPM** (800 words in 10 minutes).

**Q2: Which font is used for Hindi Steno transcription in SSC?**  
**Answer**: Mangal Font with **Remington Gail** or **Inscript** layout is provided on exam day.

**Q3: What is the maximum error percentage allowed for UR in Grade D?**  
**Answer**: General (UR) category candidates are allowed a maximum of **7% mistakes** in Grade D.

**Q4: Is backspace allowed during the computer transcription test?**  
**Answer**: Yes, you have full freedom to use backspace, delete, and arrow keys to edit your text within the allowed transcription time.
