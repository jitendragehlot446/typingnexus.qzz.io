The Border Security Force (BSF) conducts typing tests for ASI (Steno) and Head Constable (Ministerial) roles.

---

## 1. Speed Requirements

*   **English Typing**: A minimum speed of **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** (10,500 KDPH).
*   **Hindi Typing**: A minimum speed of **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** (9,000 KDPH).
*   **Duration**: **10 Minutes**.

---

## 2. Keyboard and Software Details

*   **Language**: English or Hindi.
*   **Hindi Layout**: **Mangal Font** with the **Remington Gail** layout is standard.

---

## 3. Test Interface and Rules

*   **Backspace**: Backspace is **allowed**.
*   **Printed Sheet**: You may have to type from a printed paper passage.
