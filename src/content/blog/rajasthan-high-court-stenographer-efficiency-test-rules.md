The **Rajasthan High Court Stenographer** recruitment is one of the most sought-after government exams in Rajasthan. The selection process includes **three Groups** — English Shorthand (Group A), Hindi Shorthand (Group B), and a Computer Test (Group C) that includes both a **Speed Test** and an **Efficiency Test**. Understanding every rule and mark distribution is essential to clearing this exam.

This is your definitive guide covering the complete exam pattern, shorthand rules, efficiency test syllabus, and how to practice free mock tests online.

## 2. Shorthand Test Rules (Group A & B)

The shorthand test is divided into two phases:

* **Dictation Phase**: An English passage is dictated at **80 WPM** (Group A) or a Hindi passage at **70 WPM** (Group B) for **6 minutes**. You must take shorthand notes during this phase.
* **Transcription Phase**: After dictation, you are given **50 minutes** to transcribe your shorthand notes by typing them on the computer.
* **Total Marks**: 100 marks for each language (English and Hindi separately).
* **Accuracy**: Transcription must be accurate — omissions, substitutions, and spelling errors will result in deductions.

---

## 3. Computer Speed Test Rules (Group C — Paper 1)

The speed test evaluates your typing speed on the computer:

* **Total Marks**: 50 marks
* **Duration**: 10 minutes
* **Minimum Qualifying Marks**: **20 marks** for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, **22.5 marks** for all others
* **Font for Hindi**: **Kruti Dev 010** for Hindi typing, standard font for English
* **Layout**: Remington Gail or Inscript layout for Hindi, QWERTY for English
* **Backspace**: Allowed but excessive use reduces your gross speed

---

## 4. Efficiency Test Rules & Syllabus (Group C — Paper 2)

The efficiency test is the most important part for computer-savvy candidates. It carries **50 marks** and tests your practical skills in **MS Word** and **MS Excel**:

* **Total Marks**: 50 marks (25 for Word + 25 for Excel)
* **Duration**: 10 minutes
* **Minimum Qualifying Marks**: **20 marks** for SC/ST/PwBD, **22.5 marks** for all others
* **Software**: MS Office 2007/2010 interface
* **Backspace**: Freely allowed in this section

### MS Word Tasks You Must Master:

* **Paragraph Adjustments**: Alignment, line spacing, indentation, paragraph spacing (before/after)
* **Headers and Footers**: Inserting headers/footers with page numbers, date, or custom text
* **Table Creation**: Creating tables with specific rows/columns, applying borders, cell shading, merging cells
* **Font Changes**: Changing font style, size, color, bold, italic, underline, highlighting
* **Find & Replace**: Using the Find & Replace tool to locate and change text
* **Page Setup**: Margins (including gutter), orientation, columns, and page breaks
* **Text Effects**: Emboss, Engrave, strikethrough, and special formatting

### MS Excel Tasks You Must Master:

* **Data Entry & Formatting**: Entering data, applying borders, cell fill colors, and font formatting
* **Formulas**: SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, IF — know the exact syntax
* **Sorting & Filtering**: Arranging data and applying auto-filter
* **Cell Alignment**: Merge cells, wrap text, horizontal/vertical alignment
* **Page Setup**: Print area, margins, gridlines, and headers/footers for printing

## 6. Preparation Strategy & Tips

* **Shorthand First**: Master your shorthand speed (80 WPM English / 70 WPM Hindi) before focusing on the computer test. The shorthand test carries 200 marks total.
* **Learn All MS Word Shortcuts**: Ctrl+B (Bold), Ctrl+I (Italic), Ctrl+U (Underline), Ctrl+E (Center), Ctrl+J (Justify), Ctrl+H (Find & Replace), Ctrl+Enter (Page Break).
* **Excel Formulas**: Memorize =SUM(), =AVERAGE(), =MAX(), =MIN(), =COUNT(), =IF() — these are the most frequently asked.
* **Practice Daily Under Timer**: The 10-minute time limit is strict. Build your speed by practicing daily.
* **Know the Interface**: Practice on MS Office 2007/2010 specifically — the ribbon menu layout differs from newer versions.
