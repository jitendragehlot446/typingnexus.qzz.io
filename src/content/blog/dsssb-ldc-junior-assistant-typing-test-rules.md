Welcome to the ultimate guide for the **Dsssb Ldc Junior Assistant** typing examination. If you are preparing for this competitive test, understanding the exact typing speed requirements and error calculation is crucial. Here are the official rules and links to practice.

---

## 1. Official Speed Criteria

According to the official notification, candidates must achieve a minimum English typing speed of **35 WPM** and a Hindi typing speed of **30 WPM**. The test is typically conducted for exactly **10 mins** on a computer.

---

## 2. Font Specifications (Mangal/Remington)

For English typing, the standard QWERTY keyboard is used. However, for Hindi typing, candidates must use the **Mangal Font**. You will generally have the option to select either the Remington Gail or Inscript layout.

---

## 3. Rules on Accuracy and Mistakes

The marking scheme heavily penalizes errors. While backspace is enabled, relying on it too much is dangerous. Any misspelled word or extra space counts as an error, so strive for 95%+ accuracy.
