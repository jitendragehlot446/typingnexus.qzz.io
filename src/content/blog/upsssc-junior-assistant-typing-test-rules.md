The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) conducts a mandatory **Typing Skill Test** for the recruitment of Junior Assistants and Clerical posts.

The test is qualifying in nature, meaning your typing marks won't be added to the final merit list, but passing it is absolutely mandatory. You must qualify in both Hindi and English typing to clear this stage. You can **[practice with exam-matching paragraphs](https://typingnexus.in)** for free.

Here is a detailed breakdown of the UPSSSC Junior Assistant typing speed requirements, keyboard layouts, backspace rules, and evaluation criteria.

---

## 1. Speed & Time Requirements

Candidates are tested in both Hindi and English typing:

*   **English Typing Speed**: Minimum **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Hindi Typing Speed**: Minimum **25 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Duration**: **5 minutes** for each language.
*   **Target Words**: You must type at least **150 correct words** in English and **125 correct words** in Hindi within the 5-minute limit.
*   **Evaluation**: The test is bilingual and qualifying only. Failing in even one language leads to immediate disqualification.

---

## 2. Keyboard Layout and Font Choices

UPSSSC is relatively flexible compared to other state exams, offering choices for Hindi typing:

*   **Hindi Font**: **Unicode Mangal Font** is mandatory.
*   **Hindi Keyboard Layout**: Candidates can choose between:
    1.  **Remington Gail Keyboard Layout** (derived from the typewriter layout, indic input 3 tool).
    2.  **Inscript Keyboard Layout** (standardized layout, standard Windows IME).
*   **English Layout**: Standard QWERTY layout.

---

## 3. Backspace and Navigation Rules

*   **Backspace is Allowed**: The backspace key and delete keys are fully functional during the exam. You can correct spelling errors.
*   **Arrow Keys**: Left, right, up, and down arrow keys are enabled to help navigate the typed text.
*   **Auto-Scroll**: The text passage is displayed in a split-screen pane, and the text area does not auto-scroll. Candidates must scroll down manually using the mouse or keyboard when necessary.

> [!WARNING]
   > Even though backspace is permitted, the 5-minute duration is very short. Excessive use of the backspace key will destroy your typing rhythm and cause you to miss the WPM threshold. Focus on typing accurately on your first attempt.

---

## 4. Net Speed and Error Calculation

UPSSSC calculates your speed based on the net words typed.

**Net Speed Formula:**
```text
Net Speed (WPM) = (Total Words Typed - Incorrect Words) / 5 Minutes
```

To pass, your calculated **Net Speed** must be at least 25 WPM in Hindi and 30 WPM in English. If you make too many errors, your net speed will drop, leading to disqualification.

---

## 5. Tips to Pass the UPSSSC Typing Test

1.  **Select Your Layout Early**: If you already know Krutidev, practice on the **Mangal Remington Gail** layout. If starting from scratch, learn **Inscript** as it has a lower error rate.
2.  **Build Stamina for 5-Minute Sprints**: A 5-minute test requires intense focus. Since it is shorter than standard 10-minute tests, a slow start can be fatal. Maintain a fast, accurate pace right from the first second.
3.  **Simulate the Exam Environment**: Practice on standard desktop keyboards with deep key travel. You can **[practice with timed paragraph simulators](https://typingnexus.in)** to evaluate your net speed with the exact 5-minute setting.
