## 2. Why You Should AVOID Expensive Mechanical Keyboards for Exam Practice

Many gaming aspirants buy expensive Blue-switch mechanical keyboards (₹2,500+). **This is a huge mistake for government exam candidates**:

1. **Zero Mechanical Keyboards in Exam Halls**: No government recruitment board provides mechanical keyboards. All centers use budget membrane keyboards.
2. **False Speed Advantage**: Mechanical blue switches trigger with minimal actuation force. When you sit in the real exam hall with a stiff membrane keyboard, your speed will drop by 8 to 12 WPM!
3. **Practice on What You Will Test on**: Always train on a ₹400–₹600 membrane keyboard.

## 3. Checklist: What to Look for When Buying a Practice Keyboard

* [ ] **Full 104-Key Layout**: Must include a full dedicated Numeric Keypad (NumPad) on the right side for Alt codes.
* [ ] **Wired USB Connection**: Avoid Bluetooth delay; wired keyboards offer zero millisecond input latency.
* [ ] **Standard Enter Key Shape**: Look for a horizontal or standard inverted-L Enter key matching standard keyboards.
* [ ] **Prominent F & J Bumps**: Ensure the tactile homing bumps on the `F` and `J` keys are clearly felt by your index fingers.
