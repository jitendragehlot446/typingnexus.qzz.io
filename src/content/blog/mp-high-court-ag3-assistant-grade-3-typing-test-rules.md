The **Madhya Pradesh High Court (MPHRC) Assistant Grade-3 (AG-3), Stenographer, and Junior Judicial Assistant (JJA)** recruitment is one of the most sought-after judicial clerical exams in Central India.

Unlike central exams that use Unicode Mangal, the MP High Court Mains typing examination has traditionally tested candidates on **Hindi typing in KrutiDev 010 / DevLys 010 (or Remington Gail) with a strict 350-word paragraph format**.

In this guide, we break down the exact examination pattern, 40-mark evaluation formula, CPCT score requirements, and preparation strategy.

## 1. Exam Structure & Marking Scheme (40 Marks)

In the MP High Court AG-3 Mains Exam:

* Candidates are given a printed or on-screen legal judgment passage of **approximately 350 words**.
* **Duration**: Exactly **10 minutes**.
* **Marks Deduction Scheme**:
  * Every spelling mistake, wrong character, or omission carries a deduction of **0.50 marks (Half Mark Penalty)**.
  * If a candidate makes more than **10 to 12 errors**, their qualifying score drops below the merit threshold.

---

## 2. CPCT Score Card Requirements for MP High Court

To apply for MP High Court clerical vacancies:
1. Candidate must possess a valid **CPCT (Computer Proficiency Certification Test)** scorecard issued by MAP_IT.
2. Must have qualified in **Computer Proficiency Section (50%+)** AND **Hindi Typing Test (20+ WPM)** / English Typing Test (30+ WPM).

---

## 3. Pro Tips to Clear MP High Court AG-3 Typing Test

1. **Master KrutiDev 010 & Remington Gail Alt Codes**: Legal passages in MP High Court frequently contain words like *उच्चतम न्यायालय, प्रतिवादी, याचिकाकर्ता, धारा, शपथ-पत्र*.
2. **Practice Continuous 10-Minute Tests**: Build hand stamina to type 350 words without tiring in the last 2 minutes.
3. **Double Check Legal Punctuation**: Punctuation errors (commas, full stops, hyphens) are strictly penalized.
