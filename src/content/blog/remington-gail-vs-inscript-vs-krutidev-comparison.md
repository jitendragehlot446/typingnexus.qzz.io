## 1. Deep Dive into the 3 Hindi Layouts

### A. Mangal Font (Remington Gail Layout)
* **What it is**: A modern Unicode layout that retains the traditional typewriter key arrangements.
* **Why it is popular**: Candidates who previously learned KrutiDev can switch to Remington Gail in less than 2 days because 95% of key placements are identical.
* **Key Feature**: Vowel matras like Chhoti-I (`ि`) are typed **before** the consonant (Visual order: `f` + `k` = `कि`), but the software stores it in correct Unicode sequence.
* **Major Exams**: SSC (CGL, CHSL), UP Police (ASI, Computer Operator), Patna High Court, Allahabad High Court, BSSC, DSSSB.

### B. Mangal Font (Inscript Layout)
* **What it is**: The official standard layout standardized by the Government of India for all Indian languages.
* **Key Design**: All **Vowels (स्वर)** are mapped to the Left Hand, and all **Consonants (व्यंजन)** are mapped to the Right Hand.
* **Why it is powerful**: Inscript does NOT require memorizing numeric Alt codes. All conjuncts (like `क्र`, `द्ध`, `ट्र`, `ह्र`) are naturally formed using the **Halant (`d` key)**.
* **Major Exams**: NTA Exams, IGNOU, Central Universities, SSC (Option provided alongside Remington).

### C. KrutiDev 010 / DevLys 010 (Legacy Non-Unicode Fonts)
* **What it is**: Non-Unicode legacy TrueType fonts popular in North Indian state government offices.
* **Limitation**: KrutiDev text cannot be read on modern web browsers or mobile devices without installing the specific `.ttf` font file.
* **Major Exams**: State district courts, specific typing institutes, MP High Court, and select state board clerk exams.

## 3. Which Layout Should You Learn? (Decision Guide)

### Choose **Remington Gail** if:
1. You already know basic typewriter typing or KrutiDev.
2. Your primary target exams are **SSC, UP Police, BSSC, or High Courts**.
3. You want maximum compatibility across both central and state level recruitment boards.

### Choose **Inscript** if:
1. You are a complete beginner starting Hindi typing from scratch.
2. You dislike memorizing 4-digit Alt codes (`Alt + 0216`, `Alt + 0228`).
3. You want a scientifically balanced layout with equal workload on both hands.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: Which Hindi keyboard layout is easiest for beginners?**  
**Answer**: Mangal Inscript is logically the easiest for fresh learners because all vowels are placed on the left side and consonants on the right, eliminating the need to memorize complex Alt codes.

**Answer**: Yes! Almost 95% of keystrokes in Remington Gail are identical to KrutiDev 010. You will feel comfortable within 2–3 days of practice.
