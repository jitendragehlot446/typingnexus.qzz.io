The Supreme Court of India (SCI) conducts a mandatory **Computer Typing Test** for the recruitment of Junior Court Assistants (JCAs).

This test is qualifying in nature, but because it features a very strict error calculation method, it is one of the most challenging segments of the exam. Below is a detailed breakdown of the speed thresholds, backspace rules, and interface features.

---

## 1. Speed and Duration Requirements

*   **Language**: **English only** (there is no Hindi typing option for this post).
*   **Minimum Speed**: **35 WPM** (Words Per Minute).
*   **Duration**: **10 minutes**.
*   **Keystroke Calculation**: Speed is calculated based on the standard 5-character formula (5 keystrokes = 1 word).

---

## 2. Keyboard Layout and Settings

*   **Keyboard Layout**: Standard English QWERTY keyboard.
*   **Fonts**: Standard system fonts (like Arial or Times New Roman).
*   **Systems**: High-quality membrane desktop keyboards. Avoid preparing exclusively on soft laptop keyboards, as the keys in exam centers have deep key travel.

---

## 3. The Backspace and Navigation Rule

*   **Backspace is Enabled**: You **are allowed** to use the Backspace and Delete keys during the test.
*   **Navigation Allowed**: You can use the arrow keys to navigate within the text to correct mistakes.
*   *Note:* Even though backspace is enabled, excessive corrections consume time. Because the speed threshold is strictly checked, focus on writing correctly on the first attempt.

---

## 4. Error Margin and Evaluation Formula

The Supreme Court JCA typing test allows a **maximum error margin of 3%**. 

### The Penalty Mechanism:
*   If your mistakes exceed the 3% threshold, the additional mistakes are heavily penalized.
*   A mistake count exceeding the threshold can lead to direct disqualification, even if your gross speed is much higher than 35 WPM.
*   **Calculation Formula**:
    
    ```text
    Net Speed = [Gross Words - (Mistakes exceeding 3% limit x 10)] / 10 Minutes
    ```

---

## 5. Interface Features

*   **Plain Text Interface**: The test utilizes a basic interface where the target passage is displayed in the upper box, and you type in the lower input box.
*   **No Highlighting**: There is **no highlighting** of the active word, and errors are not flagged in red during typing.
*   **No Auto-Scroll**: The screen does not scroll automatically. You must manually scroll the passage using the mouse or scroll bar as you type down the text.

---

## 6. Tips to Pass the Supreme Court JCA Typing Test

1.  **Practice on Legal Passages**: The Supreme Court typing passages are heavy on legal terminology, judgments, and legal drafts. Practice typing court orders rather than general news stories.
2.  **Aim for 40+ WPM at Home**: To comfortably qualify on the actual exam day under pressure and with complex legal terms, target a practice speed of 40–45 WPM.
3.  **Simulate the Plain Interface**: Disable highlighting options on your practice software. Learn to read from a static text block and type below it. You can **[configure mock exams matching this interface](https://typingnexus.in)**.
