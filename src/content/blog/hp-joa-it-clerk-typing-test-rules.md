The Himachal Pradesh Public Service Commission (HPPSC / HPSSC) conducts the **Junior Office Assistant (JOA IT)** and **Clerk** recruitment exams.

The selection process includes a mandatory computer-based **Typing Test**. The test is qualifying in nature, meaning your typing score won't be added to the final merit list, but passing it is mandatory to secure the post. You can **[practice with HP JOA IT mock tests](https://typingnexus.in)** for free.

Below is a detailed review of the HP JOA IT and Clerk typing speed requirements, keyboard layouts, backspace rules, and evaluation criteria.

---

## 1. Speed & Duration Requirements

Candidates can choose to take the test in English or Hindi:

*   **English Typing Speed**: Minimum **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Hindi Typing Speed**: Minimum **25 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Test Duration**: **10 minutes**.
*   **Target Keystrokes**: To qualify, you must achieve approximately **9,000 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH)** in English or **7,500 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH)** in Hindi.
*   **Word Calculation**: In official speed calculations, 1 word is defined as **5 key depressions** (including spaces and punctuation).

---

## 2. Keyboard Layout and Font Choices

The exam environment provides standard configurations for both languages:

*   **English Layout**: Standard QWERTY keyboard layout.
*   **Hindi Font**: **Unicode Mangal Font** is mandatory for modern exams.
*   **Hindi Keyboard Layout**: Candidates are generally given a choice between:
    1.  **Remington Gail Keyboard Layout** (matches typewriter layouts).
    2.  **Inscript Keyboard Layout** (standard government layout).

---

## 3. Backspace and Navigation Rules

*   **Backspace is Allowed**: The backspace key and delete keys are fully functional during both sessions. You are permitted to correct errors.
*   **Arrow Keys**: Left/Right and Up/Down arrow keys are enabled to help navigate the typed text.
*   **Standard Interface**: The source text is displayed on screen, and candidates type in a text box directly below the passage.

> [!WARNING]
   > While backspace is permitted, using it too frequently will slow down your speed. Focus on typing accurately on your first attempt. Every correction costs valuable seconds.

---

## 4. Evaluation and Net Speed Calculation

HP JOA IT evaluates candidates based on **Net Speed (WPM)**, which deducts penalties for uncorrected errors from the gross speed.

**Net WPM Formula:**
```text
Net Speed (WPM) = [(Total Keystrokes Typed - Errors Penalty) / 5] / 10 Minutes
```

To qualify, your **Net Speed** must be at least 30 WPM in English or 25 WPM in Hindi. If you type fast but commit too many uncorrected errors, your net speed may fall below the threshold, leading to disqualification.

---

## 5. Tips to Clear the HP JOA IT & Clerk Typing Test

1.  **Aim for a Safety Margin**: Aim for a speed of **35+ WPM in English** or **30+ WPM in Hindi** during your practice sessions at home. This will provide a buffer against exam-day nerves and unfamiliar keyboards.
2.  **Practice on Membrane Keyboards**: Exam centers use standard membrane desktop keyboards with deep key travel. Avoid practicing exclusively on soft laptop keyboards.
3.  **Focus on Accuracy First**: Try to keep your accuracy above 95%. This minimizes the need to use the Backspace key, saving time and keeping your typing rhythm steady. You can **[practice with timed paragraph simulators](https://typingnexus.in)** to monitor your progress.
