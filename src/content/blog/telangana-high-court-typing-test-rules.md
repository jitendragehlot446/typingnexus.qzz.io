Cracking the **Telangana High Court** requires not just academic knowledge, but also lightning-fast typing skills. Below is a detailed explanation of the official typing test rules, speed benchmarks, and keyboard layouts.

---

## 1. Typing Speed and Duration Rules

According to the official notification, candidates must achieve a minimum English typing speed of **30 WPM** and a Hindi typing speed of **25 WPM**. The test is typically conducted for exactly **10 mins** on a computer.

---

## 2. Keyboard Layout and Fonts

For English typing, the standard QWERTY keyboard is used. However, for Hindi typing, candidates must use the **Mangal Font**. You will generally have the option to select either the Remington Gail or Inscript layout.

---

## 3. Backspace and Marking Rules

The marking scheme heavily penalizes errors. While backspace is enabled, relying on it too much is dangerous. Any misspelled word or extra space counts as an error, so strive for 95%+ accuracy.
