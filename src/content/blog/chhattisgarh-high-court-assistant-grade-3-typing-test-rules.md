The **High Court of Chhattisgarh (Bilaspur)** conducts the Skill Test for **Assistant Grade-3 (AG-3), DEO, and Steno** posts as Phase-II of the recruitment process.

In Chhattisgarh High Court, the typing test carries **50 Marks (Hindi Typing - 50 Marks / English Typing - 50 Marks)** and carries substantial weightage in the final merit list!

In this guide, we explain the speed criteria, allowed keyboard layouts, marking scheme, and preparation tips for Chhattisgarh High Court.

## 1. Skill Test Marking Scheme & Evaluation (100 Marks Total)

The skill test comprises two distinct 10-minute sessions:

1. **Hindi Typing Test (50 Marks)**:
   * A passage of approximately **250 words** to be typed within **10 minutes**.
   * Deduction: **0.50 marks** deducted for every mistake (spelling, omission, punctuation).
2. **English Typing Test (50 Marks)**:
   * A passage of approximately **300 words** to be typed within **10 minutes**.
   * Deduction: **0.50 marks** deducted for every mistake.
