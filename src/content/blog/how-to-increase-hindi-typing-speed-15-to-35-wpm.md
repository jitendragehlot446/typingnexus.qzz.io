## 1. The 3 Biggest Reasons Candidates Get Stuck at 18 WPM

1. **Looking Down for Shift Keys**: Searching with your eyes every time you type uppercase characters (like `Shift + k` for `ज्ञ` or `Shift + l` for `थ`) causes a 1-second pause per word.
2. **Hesitation on Halants (्)**: Pausing to think how to make half-letters (e.g. `क` + `्` = `क्`).
3. **Backspace Addiction**: Correcting mistakes instead of maintaining a steady, continuous typing cadence.
