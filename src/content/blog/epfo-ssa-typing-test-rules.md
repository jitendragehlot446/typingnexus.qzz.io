The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) conducts a mandatory **Data Entry Skill Test (DEST)** for the recruitment of Social Security Assistants (SSAs).

The test is qualifying in nature but essential to secure your final selection. Below is a detailed review of the EPFO SSA typing speed requirements, keyboard layouts, backspace rules, and evaluation criteria.

---

## 1. Speed and Key Depression Requirements

EPFO measures typing speed in words per minute (WPM), which corresponds to a specific keystroke volume:

*   **English Typing Speed**: **35 WPM** (approx. **10,500 KDPH** - Key Depressions Per Hour).
*   **Hindi Typing Speed**: **30 WPM** (approx. **9,000 KDPH** - Key Depressions Per Hour).
*   **Test Duration**: **10 minutes**.
*   **Keystroke Calculation**: Speed is calculated based on an average of 5 key depressions per word.

---

## 2. Default Language and Selection

*   **Default Option**: The skill test is conducted in **English** by default.
*   **Hindi Choice**: Candidates who wish to take the test in Hindi must specify this preference at the examination center. Once confirmed, the language option cannot be modified.

---

## 3. Keyboard Layout and Fonts

*   **English**: Standard QWERTY keyboard layout.
*   **Hindi Font**: **Unicode Mangal Font**.
*   **Hindi Layout**: **Inscript Keyboard Layout** is used.
*   *Warning:* Unlike state-level exams that use Remington Gail, the EPFO Hindi exam uses the standard **Inscript** layout. Ensure you practice on Inscript if attempting Hindi.

---

## 4. Backspace Rule and Interface

*   **Backspace is Allowed**: You **are permitted** to use the backspace and delete keys to correct typos during the typing test.
*   **Split-Screen Interface**: The source passage is displayed in a box on the upper half of the screen, and you must type the text in the text input area on the lower half.
*   **No Auto-Scroll**: Be prepared to manually scroll down the passage using the mouse wheel or scrollbar to read the full text.

---

## 5. Tips to Pass the EPFO SSA DEST

1.  **Practice on Inscript Layout**: If you are typing in Hindi, make sure you transition to Inscript. Most candidates make the mistake of practicing on Remington Gail or Krutidev.
2.  **Aim for Higher Speeds at Home**: Aim for a consistent speed of **40+ WPM in English** or **35+ WPM in Hindi** at home. This provides a safety margin for exam-day stress and unfamiliar keyboards.
3.  **Optimize Backspace Usage**: While backspace is allowed, using it continuously interrupts your typing rhythm and slows down your overall net speed.
