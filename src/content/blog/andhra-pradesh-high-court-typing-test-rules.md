The High Court of Andhra Pradesh recruits candidates for the posts of **Typist** and **Copyist**. Since these roles demand heavy, fast, and accurate typing of legal documents, the typing test standards are exceptionally high compared to other clerical jobs.

Below are the comprehensive rules and speed thresholds for the AP High Court Typing Test.

---

## 1. High Speed Requirement

The AP High Court demands a much higher speed than standard SSC or state exams:

*   **English Speed Requirement**: A minimum of **45 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Test Duration**: Usually **10 minutes**.
*   **Language**: Predominantly conducted in English, though Telugu typing may be required for specific local posts.

Achieving 45 WPM in a stressful exam environment requires candidates to consistently hit 50-55 WPM during their home practice sessions.

---

## 2. Test Interface and Rules

*   **Screen Display**: The passage is shown on the top half of the screen, and candidates type in the text box below.
*   **Backspace**: Backspace is **allowed** to rectify errors. However, at a requirement of 45 WPM, using backspace frequently will destroy your speed. Touch typing with 98% accuracy is essential.
*   **Scroll Bar**: Auto-scrolling is typically not provided; you must manually scroll the top passage text using your mouse once you reach the bottom of the visible area.

---

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

The High Court is extremely strict regarding errors in legal drafts:

*   **Errors**: Missing a word, adding an extra word, spelling mistakes, and incorrect punctuation are considered full mistakes.
*   **Penalty**: A high number of mistakes will drastically reduce your Net WPM. If your Net WPM falls below 45, you will be disqualified.
