The Madhya Pradesh **Computer Proficiency Certification Test (CPCT)** is a mandatory certification exam for candidates seeking clerical, data entry, and patwari posts in MP state government departments.

The exam consists of two parts: a multiple-choice question (MCQ) section and a computer-based **Typing Test**. The CPCT scorecard is valid for **7 years**. You can **[practice with MP CPCT mock tests](https://typingnexus.in)** for free.

Below is a detailed review of the MP CPCT typing speed requirements, keyboard layouts, backspace rules, and evaluation criteria.

---

## 1. Speed & Scorecard Benchmarks

CPCT measures typing speed in Words Per Minute (WPM) and awards scorecards based on performance levels:

*   **English Typing Speed (Mandatory)**: Minimum **30 WPM** to pass.
*   **Hindi Typing Speed (Mandatory)**: Minimum **20 WPM** to pass.
*   **Test Duration**: **15 minutes** for each language.
*   **Target Words**: Candidates must achieve at least **450 words** in English and **300 words** in Hindi during the 15-minute test.

---

## 2. Keyboard Layout and Font Rules

The Hindi section utilizes Unicode fonts, allowing candidates to select their preferred layout:

*   **Hindi Font**: **Unicode Mangal Font** is mandatory.
*   **Hindi Keyboard Layout**: Candidates can choose between:
    1.  **Remington Gail Keyboard Layout** (uses Alt-codes for complex characters, matches typewriter settings).
    2.  **Inscript Keyboard Layout** (standardised phonetic arrangement).
*   **English Layout**: Standard QWERTY layout.

---

## 3. Backspace and Navigation Rules

*   **Backspace is Allowed**: You can use the backspace and delete keys to correct errors during the test.
*   **Split-Screen Interface**: The source text is displayed in the upper half of the screen, and candidates type in the editor in the lower half.
*   **Navigation**: Arrow keys are enabled to help navigate the typed text.
*   **No Auto-Highlighting**: The interface does not highlight incorrect characters in real-time. Proofreading must be done manually.

> [!WARNING]
   > While backspace is enabled, using it excessively during a 15-minute test will exhaust your fingers and reduce your final net speed. Focus on maintaining a steady rhythm and typing correctly on the first try.

---

## 4. Evaluation and Scoring Categories

CPCT calculates speed based on Net WPM. Accuracy is factored directly into the net speed:

**Net WPM Formula:**
```text
Net Speed (WPM) = (Total Characters Typed / 5 - Incorrect Words) / 15 Minutes
```

Your score reflects your speed band:
*   **English Passing Speed**: 30–39 WPM (50% score), 40–49 WPM (60% score), 50+ WPM (70-100% score).
*   **Hindi Passing Speed**: 20–29 WPM (50% score), 30–39 WPM (60% score), 40+ WPM (70-100% score).

---

## 5. Tips to Clear the MP CPCT Typing Test

1.  **Practice Remington Gail if familiar with Krutidev**: The key positions match Krutidev 010. If starting fresh, choose **Inscript** because it is mathematically optimized and has less key travel.
2.  **Practice 15-Minute Mock Tests**: CPCT is a long-duration typing test. Finger fatigue is common after 10 minutes. Practice 15-minute sessions to build finger strength and focus.
3.  **Accuracy is Crucial**: Uncorrected errors lead to heavy speed reductions. Prioritize a slow, steady, accurate pace over fast, error-prone bursts.
