## 1. Daily 90-Minute Practice Structure

Do NOT do your 90 minutes in one continuous session. Split it into two 45-minute blocks (Morning & Evening):

```text
Morning Session (45 Mins) : Accuracy & Muscle Memory Drills
└── 10 Mins : Finger Warm-up & Alphabet Row Drills (A to Z)
└── 20 Mins : Word-level accuracy drills (No backspace allowed)
└── 15 Mins : Difficult words & Symbol/Alt-code practice

Evening Session (45 Mins) : Real Exam Paragraph Simulation
└── 10 Mins : Warm-up paragraph (3-minute timer)
└── 25 Mins : Two Full-length 10-minute timed mock tests
└── 10 Mins : Error review & Retyping misspelled words
```

## 2. Week-by-Week Action Plan

### Week 1 (Days 1 to 7): Touch Typing & Home Row Mastery
* **Golden Rule**: **Never look down at the keyboard!** Cover your hands with a light towel or piece of paper if you catch yourself glancing at the keys.
* **F & J Bump Indicator**: Keep your index fingers resting on `F` and `J` (which have physical raised tactile bumps).
* **Target**: Build involuntary finger-key memory so your fingers find keys automatically.

### Week 2 (Days 8 to 14): Shift Keys, Capitalization & Alt Codes
* **Opposite Shift Rule**: If typing a capital letter with your right hand (like `P`), press the **Left Shift** key with your left pinky, and vice-versa.
* **Hindi Alt Codes**: Memorize the top 10 most common Alt codes (`क्र`, `द्ध`, `ट्र`, `ह्र`, `द्व`, `द्य`, `ष्ट`).
* **Punctuation Drills**: Practice sentences containing commas, full stops, hyphens, and quotation marks.

### Week 3 (Days 15 to 21): Speed Pacing & The "Backspace Detox"
* **The Backspace Detox**: For 3 days in Week 3, disable the backspace key entirely during practice. This forces your brain to verify every keystroke before pressing, instantly rocketing your accuracy from 88% to 97%.
* **Rhythm Typing**: Type like a metronome — maintain a steady, uniform beat rather than bursting fast and stalling.

### Week 4 (Days 22 to 30): Full-Length Exam Hall Simulations
* **Exact Exam Timers**: Practice with full **10-minute and 15-minute timers** on [Typing Nexus](https://typingnexus.in/practice-exams).
* **Stamina Conditioning**: A 15-minute typing test demands intense forearm endurance. Completing 2 full mocks back-to-back prevents finger cramp on exam day.
* **Pressure Testing**: Turn on background typing audio to simulate exam center noise.

## 3. Top 3 Golden Rules for Rapid Speed Gains

1. **Accuracy Breeds Speed**: If you practice fast with 85% accuracy, you reinforce errors. If you practice at 98% accuracy, your speed will naturally increase by 1 to 2 WPM every single day.
2. **Posture & Ergonomics**: Sit straight with elbows at a 90-degree angle and wrists slightly elevated (not resting heavily on the desk).
3. **Daily Consistency Over Marathon Sessions**: 1 hour of daily practice for 30 days is 10x more effective than practicing 6 hours on weekends only.
