The Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) conducts the **Clerk / LDC (Lower Division Clerk)** recruitment exam.

The selection process includes a mandatory computer-based **Typing Test** in Hindi. While the speed requirement of 25 WPM is standard, the JSSC typing test is notorious for having one of the most brutal evaluation standards in India: a **98% mandatory accuracy rate** (maximum 2% errors allowed). Below is a detailed breakdown of the JSSC Clerk typing speed requirements, keyboard layouts, backspace rules, and evaluation criteria.

---

## 1. Speed & Accuracy Requirements

The typing test is conducted in **Hindi** on a computer:

*   **Minimum Passing Speed**: **25 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Test Duration**: **10 minutes**.
*   **Target Words**: You must type at least **250 words** correctly within the 10-minute limit.
*   **Mandatory Accuracy**: **98%** (Maximum **2% errors** are allowed. Committing even a single error beyond the 2% cap leads to immediate disqualification, regardless of your typing speed).

---

## 2. Keyboard Layout and Font Rules

The exam has strict specifications for the Hindi typing environment:

*   **Hindi Font**: **Unicode Mangal Font** is mandatory.
*   **Hindi Keyboard Layout**: Candidates must use the **Remington Gail keyboard layout** (using the Indic Input 3 tool).
*   **Not Permitted**: Inscript, Devlys, or Krutidev layouts are **NOT** permitted in this test.

---

## 3. Backspace and Navigation Rules

*   **Backspace is Allowed**: The backspace and delete keys are fully functional, allowing you to correct spelling mistakes during the test.
*   **Split-Screen Interface**: The source passage is displayed in the upper box on the screen, and you must type the text in the text input box in the lower half.
*   **No Auto-Highlighting**: The interface does not highlight incorrect characters in real-time. Proofreading must be done manually.

> [!WARNING]
   > While backspace is permitted, the 98% accuracy threshold means you cannot afford to leave any typos uncorrected. However, correcting too many errors with backspace will slow down your WPM. Prioritize typing slowly and accurately on your first attempt.

---

## 4. Evaluation and Error Calculation

JSSC calculates speed based on Net Words Typed. Accuracy is factored directly into the net speed:

**Net Speed Formula:**
```text
Net Speed (WPM) = (Total Words Typed - Incorrect Words) / 10 Minutes
```

To pass, your calculated **Net Speed** must be at least 25 WPM. If you type fast but commit too many uncorrected errors, your net speed may fall below the threshold, leading to disqualification.

---

## 5. Tips to Clear the JSSC Clerk Typing Test

1.  **Transition to Remington Gail early**: If you already know Krutidev, practice on the **Mangal Remington Gail** layout.
2.  **Aim for a Safety Margin**: Aim for a speed of **30+ WPM** during your practice sessions at home. This will provide a buffer against exam-day nerves and unfamiliar keyboards.
3.  **Focus on Accuracy First**: Try to keep your accuracy above 98%. This minimizes the need to use the Backspace key, saving time and keeping your typing rhythm steady. You can **[practice with timed paragraph simulators](https://typingnexus.in)** to monitor your progress.
