Preparing for the **JVVNL (Jaipur Vidyut Vitran Nigam Limited) Junior Assistant / Commercial Assistant-II** exam? The Phase II **Computer Proficiency Test (CPT)** is where most candidates stumble. The efficiency test portion evaluates your real-world skills in **MS Word** and **MS Excel** — and it carries **25 marks** for each language (Hindi and English), totaling up to **100 marks** across all CPT sections.

Here's your complete guide to the efficiency test rules, syllabus, and how to practice free mock tests online.

---

## 1. JVVNL CPT Efficiency Test Pattern & Marks

The JVVNL CPT is divided into four sections — two for typing speed and two for efficiency. The efficiency test structure is:

* **Hindi Efficiency Test**: 25 marks (MS Word & Excel tasks in Hindi)
* **English Efficiency Test**: 25 marks (MS Word & Excel tasks in English)
* **Duration per section**: 10 minutes each
* **Minimum Qualifying Marks**: **9 marks out of 25** in each individual section (mandatory — falling below in any one section leads to disqualification)
* **Software Interface**: MS Office 2007/2010 environment

You will be given an on-screen document with specific formatting instructions to execute within the time limit.

---

## 2. MS Word Efficiency Test Syllabus for JVVNL

The Word section tests practical document formatting skills. Key areas include:

* **Text Formatting**: Font style, size, color, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, highlighting, and Emboss/Engrave effects
* **Paragraph Settings**: Alignment (left, center, right, justify), line spacing, paragraph spacing (before/after), indentation, and hanging indent
* **Page Setup**: Margins (top, bottom, left, right, gutter), page orientation, page size, and columns
* **Headers & Footers**: Inserting headers/footers with page numbers, date, or custom text
* **Tables**: Creating tables with specified rows and columns, applying borders and shading, merging/splitting cells
* **Editing**: Find and replace operations, change case, inserting symbols and special characters
* **Insert Elements**: Inserting watermarks, shapes, images, horizontal lines, and page breaks

---

## 3. MS Excel Efficiency Test Syllabus for JVVNL

The Excel portion evaluates spreadsheet and data management skills:

* **Data Entry & Formatting**: Entering data in cells, applying font formatting, cell borders, and fill colors
* **Formulas & Functions**: SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, COUNTA, and IF functions
* **Cell Alignment**: Horizontal/vertical alignment, wrap text, merge cells, and text rotation
* **Sorting & Filtering**: Sorting data alphabetically or numerically, applying auto-filter
* **Number Formatting**: Currency, percentage, date, and decimal formatting
* **Page Layout for Printing**: Setting print area, adjusting margins, page orientation, gridlines, and headers/footers

## 5. Key Rules & Preparation Tips

* **Precision Over Speed**: The efficiency test is about accurate formatting, not fast typing. One wrong border or incorrect spacing costs marks.
* **Master MS Office Shortcuts**: Learn Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+U, Ctrl+E, Ctrl+J, Ctrl+H, Ctrl+1 (single spacing), Ctrl+5 (1.5 spacing), and Ctrl+2 (double spacing).
* **Font for Hindi**: **Kruti Dev 010 / DevLys 010** is commonly used for Hindi formatting tasks. For English, standard **Calibri** or **Times New Roman** is used.
* **Practice Both Languages**: Since Hindi and English efficiency tests are separately marked and each requires minimum 9 marks, practice both equally.
* **Time Management**: Each test is only 10 minutes. Practicing under timed conditions is essential.
