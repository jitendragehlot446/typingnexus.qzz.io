## 1. Word Highlighting vs Plain Text Mode

* **What is Word Highlighting?**: In casual typing games, the word you are currently typing turns green or gets a colored background box.
* **Exam Reality (SSC & High Courts)**: Most official government typing software (including SSC and Courts) **does NOT highlight the active word**. You see a master passage in the upper pane and a blank typing box in the lower pane.
* **How to Train**: Train your eyes to read 2 to 3 words ahead in the master passage without relying on color highlights. Practice on [Typing Nexus](https://typingnexus.in) with "Highlighting OFF" to simulate the exact SSC environment.

## 2. Auto-Scrolling vs Manual Scrolling (Mouse Navigation)

* **The Problem**: A 10-minute or 15-minute passage contains 350 to 500 words, which does not fit entirely on one screen.
* **Manual Scroll Bar**: In SSC and High Court interfaces, as you type towards the bottom of the visible box, **the screen does NOT scroll automatically**.
* **What You Must Do**: When you reach the last visible line, quickly use your mouse scroll wheel or click the scrollbar on the right to bring the next paragraph lines into view.
* **Pro Tip**: Use the **Page Down** key or mouse wheel with your right hand without removing your left hand from the home row to save crucial seconds.

## 3. Retyping Rules: What to Do If the Passage Finishes Early?

Many fast typists (45+ WPM) finish a 300-word passage in 7 minutes and wonder: *"Should I stop typing, or should I re-type the passage from the beginning?"*

* **Official Rule (SSC & RRB NTPC)**: If you complete the entire passage before the 10/15-minute countdown ends, you **MUST press Space / Enter and begin retyping the passage from the very first word!**
* **Why Retyping Matters**: Gross and Net speed calculations are computed over the **entire duration**. If you stop typing with 3 minutes remaining, your speed will be divided by the full test time (e.g. 15 mins), causing your calculated WPM to crash!
* **Exception (Fixed Paragraph Exams)**: In certain judicial exams where only 1 fixed passage of 300 words is graded, you are instructed to stop and review your text. Always read the on-screen instructions carefully before starting.

## 4. Prohibited Keys & Shortcut Restrictions

In all secure exam browsers (TCS iON iLEON Browser):

* `Ctrl + C`, `Ctrl + V`, `Ctrl + X` (Copy/Paste/Cut): **Completely Blocked**.
* `Tab Key`: Usually disabled or acts as form control navigation (do not press Tab to indent).
* `Alt + Tab` / `Windows Key`: Attempting to switch windows will trigger an instant security flag or terminal lock.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: What happens if I finish typing the text before 10 minutes in SSC CHSL/CGL?**  
**Answer**: You must press the spacebar once and retype the passage from the beginning until the countdown timer expires to ensure your speed score is accurately maximized.

**Q2: Does the mouse work during the government typing test?**  
**Answer**: Yes, the mouse is active and can be used to scroll up/down and reposition the text cursor within the typing box.

**Q3: Is word highlighting available in SSC typing skill tests?**  
**Answer**: No, SSC typing tests feature a static plain-text master pane without live word highlighting.

**Q4: Can we use the Enter key at the end of every line?**  
**Answer**: In continuous typing software, you should only press the **Spacebar** between words. Press the **Enter key** only when moving to a distinct new paragraph indicated in the master passage.
