The Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) and Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) conduct rigorous recruitment skill tests for **Head Constable Ministerial (HCM)** posts.

Because ministerial head constables handle administrative files, service books, and official correspondence in border battalions, the recruitment board strictly tests your computer keyboard accuracy and sustained speed.

In this guide, we cover the exact speed benchmarks, font requirements, error calculation methods, and practice tips for both **ITBP HCM and SSB HCM** examinations.

## 1. Speed Calculation & 5-Keystroke Standard

* **1 Standard Word = 5 Keystrokes** (including spacebar and punctuation marks).
* To qualify in **English (35 WPM in 10 minutes)**, you must register at least **1750 key depressions** with minimal errors.
* To qualify in **Hindi (30 WPM in 10 minutes)**, you must register at least **1500 key depressions**.

---

## 2. Mistake Penalties & Error Evaluation

Both ITBP and SSB enforce standard central paramilitary error deductions:

* **Omission of words** or typing wrong words is counted as a Full Mistake.
* **Spelling errors, capitalization, or extra spaces** are evaluated as Half Mistakes.
* Net speed must remain at or above **35 WPM (English) or 30 WPM (Hindi)** after error deductions.

---

## 3. Keyboard & Hardware Environment in Paramilitary Camps

* Testing is conducted in designated ITBP/SSB training centers (e.g. *Tigri Camp New Delhi, RTC Karera, Recruit Training Centers*).
* Desktops are equipped with standard full-size USB keyboards (Dell / HP / ProDot).
* A 5-minute practice session is granted before the scored test begins to verify key responsiveness.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: What typing speed is required for ITBP and SSB HCM?**  
**Answer**: 35 WPM in English OR 30 WPM in Hindi on a computer in a 10-minute continuous test.

**Q2: Is Hindi typing conducted in Mangal font or KrutiDev in ITBP/SSB?**  
**Answer**: Central paramilitary recruitment boards use Unicode Mangal font (Remington Gail and Inscript layouts).

**Q3: Does the typing test mark add to the final merit list?**  
**Answer**: No, the typing skill test is strictly qualifying in nature, but passing is mandatory to move to the medical examination stage.

**Q4: Is backspace allowed in ITBP/SSB typing tests?**  
**Answer**: Yes, the backspace key is allowed, but excessive use reduces your overall keystroke volume and net speed.
