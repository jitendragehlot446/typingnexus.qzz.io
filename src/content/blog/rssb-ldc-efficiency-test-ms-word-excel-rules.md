The **RSSB LDC (Junior Assistant / Clerk Grade-II)** recruitment Phase II includes a crucial **Computer Proficiency Test (CPT)** that tests your practical skills in MS Word and MS Excel. This efficiency test carries **25 marks** and you must score a minimum of **9 marks** to qualify. Thousands of candidates clear the written exam but fail here — don't let that be you.

This is your complete guide to understanding the rules, syllabus, and how to practice free mock tests online.

---

## 1. Efficiency Test Pattern & Marks Distribution

The RSSB LDC Phase II CPT is divided into two components. The efficiency test specifically evaluates your ability to format documents and manipulate spreadsheets:

* **Total Marks for Efficiency Test**: 25 marks
* **Duration**: 10 minutes
* **Minimum Qualifying Marks**: 9 out of 25 (mandatory — failing this disqualifies you)
* **Software Tested**: MS Word and MS Excel (typically MS Office 2007/2010 interface)

You will receive on-screen instructions asking you to perform specific formatting tasks on a given passage or spreadsheet.

---

## 2. MS Word Efficiency Test Syllabus

The MS Word portion tests your practical formatting skills. Common tasks include:

* **Text Formatting**: Bold, italic, underline, font style, font size, font color, text highlighting, and Emboss effect
* **Paragraph Formatting**: Alignment (left, right, center, justify), line spacing (single, 1.5, double, "At least" spacing), indentation, and first-line indent
* **Page Layout**: Page orientation (portrait/landscape), margins (including gutter margin), page numbering, and columns
* **Headers & Footers**: Inserting and editing headers and footers with page numbers or custom text
* **Tables**: Creating tables with specific rows/columns, applying borders, cell shading, and merging cells
* **Editing Tools**: Find and replace, toggle case (uppercase/lowercase), and inserting horizontal lines
* **Document Views**: Switching between Print Layout, Web Layout, and other views

---

## 3. MS Excel Efficiency Test Syllabus

The MS Excel portion tests your spreadsheet handling abilities:

* **Data Entry**: Entering data into specific cells accurately
* **Cell Formatting**: Font changes, borders, alignment, cell shading, number formatting (currency, percentage)
* **Basic Formulas**: SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, and IF functions
* **Sorting & Filtering**: Arranging data in ascending/descending order and applying auto-filters
* **Page Setup**: Adjusting margins, print area, headers/footers, and gridline visibility for printing

## 5. Important Rules & Tips

* **Accuracy is Everything**: Unlike speed tests where you type fast, the efficiency test rewards precision. Follow formatting instructions exactly.
* **Learn Keyboard Shortcuts**: Master shortcuts like Ctrl+B (Bold), Ctrl+I (Italic), Ctrl+U (Underline), Ctrl+H (Find & Replace), and Ctrl+E (Center Align) to save valuable time.
* **Hindi Font**: For Hindi content, **Kruti Dev 010** is typically used. For English, standard fonts like Calibri are provided.
* **Practice with Timer**: Always practice with a 10-minute timer to simulate real exam pressure.
