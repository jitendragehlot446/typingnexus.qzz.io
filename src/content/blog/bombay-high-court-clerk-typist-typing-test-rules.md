The **High Court of Judicature at Bombay (and its benches at Nagpur, Aurangabad, and Goa)** conducts recruitment for **Clerk / Typist, Peon / Hamal, and Stenographer** posts.

Candidates who qualify the preliminary screening test must appear for the **English Typing Test (40 WPM) and Marathi Typing Test (30 WPM) carrying 20 Marks each**.

In this guide, we provide a complete breakdown of the speed benchmarks, GCC-TBC certificate criteria, evaluation rules, and preparation tips.

---

## Quick Overview: Bombay High Court Key Specifications

| Parameter | Official High Court of Bombay Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **English Typing Speed** | **40 Words Per Minute (WPM)** (400 Words / 10 Mins) |
| **Marathi Typing Speed** | **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** (300 Words / 10 Mins) |
| **Typing Test Marks** | **20 Marks (English) + 20 Marks (Marathi)** |
| **Mandatory Certificates** | **GCC-TBC (Government Commercial Certificate)** |
| **Allowed Marathi Fonts** | **Unicode Mangal (Remington / Inscript) / ISM Marathi** |
| **Permissible Error** | **Up to 5% Mistakes** |

## 1. GCC-TBC Certificate Eligibility

For Bombay High Court Clerk / Typist applications:
* Candidate must possess a Government Commercial Certificate (GCC-TBC) or ITI certificate with minimum **30 WPM in Marathi / Hindi** and **40 WPM in English**.
