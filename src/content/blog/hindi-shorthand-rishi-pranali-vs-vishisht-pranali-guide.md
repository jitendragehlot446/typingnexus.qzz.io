## 1. Deep Dive into the 3 Hindi Shorthand Systems

### A. Rishi Pranali (ऋषि प्रणाली - Pitman Based)
* **Creator**: Developed by late Pt. Rishi Gopal Garg.
* **Why it dominates India**: Over 80% of typing institutes in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, and Delhi teach Rishi Pranali.
* **Key Strength**: Highly optimized **Grammalogues (शब्दचिन्ह)** and **Phraseography (वाक्यांश)** that allow candidates to write long sentences in just 2–3 pencil strokes.
* **Best Choice For**: Anyone starting stenography today, because you will easily find teachers, video lessons, dictation audios, and online communities.

### B. Vishisht Pranali (विशिष्ट प्रणाली)
* **Creator**: Developed by Dr. Gopal Datt Bisht (former Parliamentary Reporter).
* **Key Strength**: Designed specifically for high-speed parliamentary reporting (140–160 WPM). Focuses heavily on phonetic precision and vowel clarity.
* **Best Choice For**: Candidates targeting Lok Sabha / Rajya Sabha Reporter and High Court Personal Assistant vacancies.

### C. Manak Pranali (मानक प्रणाली)
* **Background**: Standardized by the Commission for Scientific and Technical Terminology (CSTT), Government of India.
* **Key Strength**: Strict adherence to standard Hindi grammar and phonetic rules.
* **Limitation**: Fewer private coaching institutes teach Manak Pranali compared to Rishi Pranali.

## 2. How to Master Transcription on Computer (The Real Exam Challenge)

In all stenographer exams, **you are graded NOT on your shorthand notebook, but on your COMPUTER TYPING TRANSCRIPTION!**

1. **Dictation Stage**: You listen to an audio dictation (80 WPM for 10 mins = 800 words, or 100 WPM for 10 mins = 1000 words) and write in your shorthand notebook with pencil.
2. **Reading Time**: You get 10 minutes to read through and mark your shorthand notes.
3. **Computer Transcription**: You type out the entire passage on a computer terminal within **50 to 65 minutes**.
4. **Typing Speed Prerequisite**: To comfortably transcribe 800 words in 50 minutes, you need a minimum **30–35 WPM Hindi computer typing speed**.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: Which Hindi shorthand system is the best for beginners?**  
**Answer**: **Rishi Pranali** is strongly recommended for beginners because study materials, online dictations, and expert teachers are universally available.

**Q2: How long does it take to learn Hindi stenography at 80 WPM?**  
**Answer**: With 2 to 3 hours of dedicated daily practice, a student can learn the complete theory and reach 80 WPM within **6 to 8 months**.

**Q3: Can I write in Rishi Pranali and type in Mangal Remington Gail?**  
**Answer**: Yes! You take dictation notes in Rishi shorthand on paper, and then type out the final Hindi text on computer in **Mangal Remington Gail** or **Inscript**.
