## 1. What Keyboards are Provided at TCS iON Exam Centers?

TCS iON centers across India do not use expensive mechanical gaming keyboards. Instead, they deploy enterprise-grade standard **USB Membrane Keyboards**:

1. **Dell KB216**: Chiclet-style with low profile keys (common in newly upgraded centers).
2. **ProDot / Intex / Quantum**: Traditional high-profile keys with deeper key travel and louder click sound.
3. **HP / Lenovo Classic Business Keyboards**: Medium key travel with textured keycaps.

> **Crucial Insight**: Because these keyboards are used daily by thousands of candidates, some keys (especially **Spacebar, Enter, Shift, and Backspace**) may feel slightly stiffer or require a firmer press than your home keyboard.

## 2. The 5-Minute Mock / Trial Window: Your Golden Opportunity

Before the real 10 or 15-minute examination starts, the exam software gives you a mandatory **5-minute Practice / Trial Session**.

**Follow this 7-Point Keyboard Checklist during the trial session:**

* [ ] **Test Spacebar**: Press the spacebar with both thumbs at the left, center, and right edges to ensure it doesn't get stuck.
* [ ] **Test Shift Keys**: Check both Left Shift and Right Shift for capitalization and symbol inputs.
* [ ] **Test Backspace Key**: Ensure it registers smoothly without requiring heavy force.
* [ ] **Test Number Row & NumPad**: Type `0` through `9` on both the top number row and numeric keypad (crucial for Alt-codes).
* [ ] **Test Corner Letters**: Specifically test `Q`, `P`, `Z`, `M`, and vowels `A`, `E`, `I`, `O`, `U`.
* [ ] **Check Keyboard Stability**: Ensure the rubber feet on the underside are intact and the keyboard does not wobble on the desk.
* [ ] **Adjust Distance**: Position the keyboard comfortably relative to the monitor before the real test begins.

## 3. How to Get a Faulty Keyboard Replaced in the Exam Hall

If any key feels stuck, unresponsive, or registers double-clicks:

1. **Act Immediately**: Raise your hand **during the Mock / Trial Session**. Do NOT wait for the actual exam timer to start!
2. **Alert the Invigilator**: Say politely: *"Sir/Madam, the Spacebar/Shift key on this keyboard is malfunctioning. Please replace the keyboard."*
3. **Wait for Test Connection**: The technical staff will replace the USB keyboard. Verify the new keyboard before confirming ready status.
4. **Official Rule**: Exam authorities are obligated to provide a fully functioning keyboard prior to the start of the scored test.

## 4. How to Practice at Home for TCS iON Conditions

1. **Avoid Soft Laptop Keyboards**: Practicing solely on shallow laptop keyboards develops light-finger habits that falter on stiffer desktop keyboards.
2. **Buy a Budget Desktop Keyboard**: Practice on a standard ₹400–₹600 membrane keyboard (like *Dell KB216* or *ProDot*) to build real finger strength.
3. **Turn Up Keyboard Volume / Ambient Sound**: In the exam hall, 100+ candidates typing simultaneously creates massive noise. Practice with background typing audio or in a busy room to build mental immunity.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: Can I bring my own keyboard to the TCS iON typing test center?**  
**Answer**: No, candidates are strictly prohibited from bringing their own peripherals. You must use the desktop and keyboard provided at the exam center.

**Q2: What should I do if a key stops working during the actual test?**  
**Answer**: Immediately raise your hand and alert the invigilator. In case of hardware failure during the active test, the center administrator will re-allocate you to a buffer terminal and re-schedule the test according to commission guidelines.

**Q3: Are mechanical keyboards provided in government typing tests?**  
**Answer**: No, all government recruitment exams use standard membrane (rubber-dome) desktop keyboards.

**Q4: Is the numeric keypad available on exam center keyboards?**  
**Answer**: Yes, all exam center keyboards are full-sized 104-key standard desktop keyboards with dedicated numeric keypads.
