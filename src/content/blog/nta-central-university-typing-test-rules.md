Cracking the **Nta Central University** requires not just academic knowledge, but also lightning-fast typing skills. Below is a detailed explanation of the official typing test rules, speed benchmarks, and keyboard layouts.

---

## 1. Speed Requirements and Time Limit

According to the official notification, candidates must achieve a minimum English typing speed of **30 WPM** and a Hindi typing speed of **25 WPM**. The test is typically conducted for exactly **10 mins** on a computer.

---

## 2. Font Specifications (Mangal/Remington)

The exam interface provides a standard QWERTY layout for English. For Hindi, the officially mandated font is **Mangal Unicode**. Ensure you practice on the Remington Gail layout as it is the most commonly provided option.

---

## 3. Error Calculation and Backspace

In most modern online typing tests for this exam, the **backspace key is allowed**. However, excessive use of backspace will slow down your gross speed. You must maintain an accuracy of at least 95% to avoid heavy penalties.
