Cracking the **Dsssb** requires not just academic knowledge, but also lightning-fast typing skills. Below is a detailed explanation of the official typing test rules, speed benchmarks, and keyboard layouts.

---

## 1. Official Speed Criteria

According to the official notification, candidates must achieve a minimum English typing speed of **35 WPM** and a Hindi typing speed of **30 WPM**. The test is typically conducted for exactly **10 mins** on a computer.

---

## 2. Keyboard Layout and Fonts

For English typing, the standard QWERTY keyboard is used. However, for Hindi typing, candidates must use the **Mangal Font**. You will generally have the option to select either the Remington Gail or Inscript layout.

---

## 3. Backspace and Marking Rules

In most modern online typing tests for this exam, the **backspace key is allowed**. However, excessive use of backspace will slow down your gross speed. You must maintain an accuracy of at least 95% to avoid heavy penalties.
