The All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) conducts the **Common Recruitment Examination (CRE)** to recruit candidates for various Group B and Group C posts. For specific professional administrative roles, passing the typing skill test is a strict requirement.

This test is qualifying in nature, but the standards are strictly enforced. Below are the specific rules and speed requirements you need to know to pass the AIIMS CRE Professional Typing Test.

---

## 1. Speed Requirements and Test Duration

Depending on your medium of choice (English or Hindi), the speed requirements are slightly different:

*   **English Typing Speed**: A minimum of **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)** on the computer. This translates to exactly 10,500 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH).
*   **Hindi Typing Speed**: A minimum of **30 Words Per Minute (WPM)** on the computer. This translates to exactly 9,000 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH).
*   **Test Duration**: The test is usually conducted for exactly **10 minutes**.

*Note: 1 Word is calculated as 5 key depressions, including spaces.*

---

## 2. Keyboard Layout and Font Specifications

AIIMS provides specific environments for Hindi typists:

*   **English Setup**: Standard QWERTY keyboard layout is provided.
*   **Hindi Setup**: The standard font used is **Mangal Font**. Candidates usually have the choice between the **Inscript** or **Remington Gail** layout. It is highly advised to practice on the Remington Gail layout if you are accustomed to the Krutidev style.

---

## 3. Backspace and Error Calculation

AIIMS follows a standard evaluation process similar to SSC:

*   **Backspace**: Backspace is **allowed** and can be used to correct errors while typing. However, using it excessively will drastically reduce your gross speed.
*   **Error Penalty**: Errors like missing a word, substituting a wrong word, or adding extra words are counted as Full Mistakes. Spacing or capitalization errors are considered Half Mistakes.
*   **Accuracy Benchmark**: You must maintain an accuracy of **at least 95%** to comfortably pass the net speed threshold.
