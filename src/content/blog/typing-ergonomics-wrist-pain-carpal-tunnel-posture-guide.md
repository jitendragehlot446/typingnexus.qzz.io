## 1. The 3 Biggest Posture Mistakes That Cause Wrist Pain

### Mistake 1: Resting Wrists Heavily on Desk or Table Edge
* **Why it damages tendons**: When you press the base of your palm firmly against a sharp wooden table or laptop edge, you compress the **Median Nerve** passing through the carpal tunnel.
* **The Fix**: Keep your wrists **lightly floating (hovering)** above the keys while typing, exactly like playing a piano.

### Mistake 2: Practicing on Bed or Sofa with a Laptop
* **The Danger**: Slouching on a bed bends your cervical spine forward and forces your wrists into an extreme upward angle, creating double tendon strain in less than 30 minutes.
* **The Fix**: Always practice on a sturdy table with a back-supporting chair.

### Mistake 3: Pounding Keys with Excessive Force
* **The Danger**: Banging keys hard with heavy finger strikes transmits repetitive shock waves into your knuckle joints.
* **The Fix**: Type with light, rhythmic taps. Modern membrane keyboards register with gentle pressure.

## 2. 5-Minute Physiotherapy Hand Stretches (Do Every 45 Minutes)

Perform these 4 simple physiotherapy exercises between practice sessions:

```text
Daily 5-Minute Hand Relief Routine:
├── 1. Prayer Stretch (30 Secs)     : Palms together at chest level, lower hands gently
├── 2. Reverse Wrist Extensor (30s)  : Extend arm forward, gently pull fingers backward
├── 3. Reverse Wrist Flexor (30s)    : Extend arm forward, gently pull fingers downward
├── 4. Finger Spiders & Fists (1 Min): Make tight fist for 3s, then splay fingers wide
└── 5. Wrist Rotations (1 Min)       : 10 clockwise and 10 anti-clockwise circles
```

## 3. Ideal Ergonomic Desk & Chair Setup

1. **Chair Height**: Adjust your chair so your elbows naturally rest level with the keyboard spacebar without lifting your shoulders.
2. **Keyboard Tilt**: Keep the keyboard flat. Do NOT pop up the little back legs of the keyboard, as backward tilt bends your wrists upward (dorsiflexion).
3. **Warm Hands Before Typing**: Cold muscles tear easily. Wash your hands with warm water or rub palms vigorously for 30 seconds before your morning practice session.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: How can I cure wrist pain caused by typing practice?**  
**Answer**: Rest your hands for 24 hours, apply a cold ice pack for 10 minutes to reduce tendon swelling, perform gentle wrist extensor stretches, and switch to a floating wrist posture.

**Q2: How many hours should I practice typing each day without hurting my hands?**  
**Answer**: The ideal duration is **60 to 90 minutes daily**, split into two 45-minute blocks with a 5-minute stretch break in between. Practicing more than 3 continuous hours without breaks increases injury risk.

**Q3: Are wrist-rest gel pads recommended for typing?**  
**Answer**: Gel pads are meant for resting your palms **when you pause**, NOT while actively typing. While actively typing, keep your wrists elevated in neutral alignment.

**Q4: What are the early warning signs of Carpal Tunnel Syndrome?**  
**Answer**: Tingling ("pins and needles"), numbness in the thumb, index, and middle fingers, and weakness when gripping small objects.
