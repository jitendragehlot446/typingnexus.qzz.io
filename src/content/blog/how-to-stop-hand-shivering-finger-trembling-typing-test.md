## 1. Why Do Hands Shiver in the Typing Exam Hall?

Hand trembling is a physiological reaction caused by three combined triggers:

1. **Adrenaline Release (Fight or Flight)**: Your body perceives the high-stakes test as danger, releasing adrenaline that causes fine muscle tremors in the fingers.
2. **Cold Examination Labs**: Exam center labs often run high air-conditioning to cool server banks. Cold ambient temperature constricts blood vessels in fingertips, reducing dexterity.
3. **Sensory Overload (The Keyboard Thunder)**: When the timer starts and 100 people simultaneously bang on keyboards, unprepared candidates panic, feeling they are lagging behind.

## 2. 5-Minute Physical Warm-Up Drills (Before Entering Hall)

Perform these simple physical drills in the waiting area 10 minutes before reporting:

### Drill 1: Palm Friction Heat Generation
* Rub both palms vigorously together for 30 seconds until you feel strong warmth.
* Place warm palms over the backs of your hands and massage down to the fingertips.

### Drill 2: Finger Clenches and Wrist Circles
* Make tight fists for 3 seconds, then spread fingers wide open. Repeat 10 times.
* Rotate both wrists clockwise 10 times and anti-clockwise 10 times to release forearm tension.

### Drill 3: Finger Tapping Cadence
* Rest your fingers on a flat surface (your lap or desk) and tap each finger individually from pinky to thumb in rhythmic sequence.

## 3. The "First 2 Minutes" Psychological Rule

The first 120 seconds determine your entire test outcome:

* **DO NOT Rush on Second 1**: When the screen displays "START", take a deep breath and start typing at **75% of your maximum speed**.
* **Focus Purely on 100% Accuracy**: Type the first 50 words with zero mistakes. Once you establish a clean rhythm and your brain registers that you are in control, the hand shivering will naturally vanish within 90 seconds!
* **Accelerate Gradually**: After minute 2, gradually let your muscle memory push your speed up to your natural 38–42 WPM cruising pace.

## 4. How to Handle the Exam Hall Noise Chaos

* **Ignore Other Candidates**: The person next to you banging their keyboard loudly is likely making dozens of typos and backspaces. Noise does not equal speed!
* **Tunnel Vision**: Look only at the 2 words directly ahead of your typing cursor. Do not look at the countdown timer until the 5-minute mark.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: How can I stop my hands from shivering during a typing test?**  
**Answer**: Warm your hands by rubbing palms before entering the hall, take 3 slow deep breaths, and type at a deliberate, slow pace for the first 90 seconds until your heart rate stabilizes.

**Q2: Are earplugs allowed inside the typing exam center?**  
**Answer**: Basic foam earplugs are permitted in some centers if inspected, but many centers disallow them. It is best to train your mental focus to ignore ambient noise without relying on earplugs.

**Q3: Does washing hands with warm water help before the exam?**  
**Answer**: Yes! Washing your hands with warm water in the restroom 15 minutes before reporting dilates blood vessels, warms finger joints, and significantly boosts finger mobility.

**Q4: What if I make multiple mistakes in the first sentence?**  
**Answer**: Pause for exactly 2 seconds, exhale completely, relax your shoulders, and resume typing steadily. A 2-second reset is far better than spiraling into consecutive errors.
