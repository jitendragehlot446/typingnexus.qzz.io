The Jammu and Kashmir Services Selection Board (JKSSB) conducts the **Junior Assistant** recruitment exam.

Unlike most government typing tests which are only qualifying, the JKSSB Junior Assistant typing test carries **10 marks weightage** in the final merit list. The remaining 90 marks are allocated to the written exam. This means achieving a higher typing speed directly increases your chances of selection. You can **[practice with JKSSB mock exams](https://typingnexus.in)** for free.

Below is a detailed breakdown of the JKSSB Junior Assistant typing speed requirements, keyboard layouts, backspace rules, and evaluation criteria.

---

## 1. Speed & Accuracy Requirements

The typing test is conducted in **English** on a computer:

*   **Minimum Passing Speed**: **35 Words Per Minute (WPM)**.
*   **Minimum Accuracy**: **90%** (your accuracy must not fall below this threshold).
*   **Test Duration**: **10 minutes**.
*   **Scoring Weightage**: The test is graded out of **10 marks**. Scaling is applied based on your net speed. For example, a candidate typing at 35 WPM will get lower marks, while a candidate typing at 60+ WPM will secure close to the maximum 10 marks.

---

## 2. Keyboard Layout and Interface

*   **Layout**: Standard QWERTY keyboard layout.
*   **Split-Screen Interface**: The source passage is displayed in the upper half of the screen, and you must type the text in the text input box in the lower half.
*   **No Auto-Highlighting**: The interface does not highlight incorrect characters in real-time. Proofreading must be done manually.

---

## 3. Backspace and Navigation Rules

*   **Backspace is Allowed**: You can use the backspace key and delete keys to edit and correct typos during the 10-minute test.
*   **Arrow Keys**: Left and right arrow keys are enabled to help navigate the typed text.
*   **Auto-Scroll**: The text passage is displayed in a split-screen pane, and the text area does not auto-scroll. Candidates must scroll down manually using the mouse or keyboard when necessary.

> [!WARNING]
   > While backspace is permitted, using it too frequently will slow down your speed. Focus on typing accurately on your first attempt. Every correction costs valuable seconds.

---

## 4. Evaluation and Speed Calculation

JKSSB evaluates candidates based on **Net Speed (WPM)**, which deducts penalties for uncorrected errors from the gross speed.

**Net WPM Formula:**
```text
Net Speed (WPM) = [(Total Keystrokes Typed - Errors Penalty) / 5] / 10 Minutes
```

To qualify, your **Net Speed** must be at least 35 WPM. If you type fast but commit too many uncorrected errors, your net speed may fall below the threshold, leading to disqualification.

---

## 5. Tips to Pass the JKSSB Junior Assistant Typing Test

1.  **Aim for a Safety Margin**: Aim for a speed of **40+ WPM** during your practice sessions at home. This will provide a buffer against exam-day nerves and unfamiliar keyboards.
2.  **Practice on Membrane Keyboards**: Exam centers use standard membrane desktop keyboards with deep key travel. Avoid practicing exclusively on soft laptop keyboards.
3.  **Focus on Accuracy First**: Try to keep your accuracy above 95%. This minimizes the need to use the Backspace key, saving time and keeping your typing rhythm steady. You can **[practice with timed paragraph simulators](https://typingnexus.in)** to monitor your progress.
